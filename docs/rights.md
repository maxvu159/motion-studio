# Rights checklist (read before choosing music, footage, fonts, logos)

Not legal advice — a practical checklist from making these videos.

## Music
- A song has two rights: the **composition** (melody, chords, beat — writers/publisher) and the
  **recording** (label). An **instrumental** removes vocals, not either right. Remakes of a famous beat
  still use the composition.
- Platforms fingerprint audio (X, YouTube, Instagram): videos get muted, blocked or claimed.
- A brand account posting a launch video is **commercial use** → needs a sync licence. For major-label
  songs that is usually impossible or very expensive.
- Safe routes: licensed libraries (Artlist, Musicbed, Epidemic Sound, Soundstripe — search "upbeat hip
  hop", "disco house", "confident"), an exclusive licence from a producer ("type beat" = new composition
  in a style — read the licence), or a commissioned track.
- For internal previews any track can be used to find the edit; swap to the licensed track before publish
  and re-run beatmap → the cut is in beats, so it re-times with the new BPM.

## Footage, stills, memes
- News clips, film scenes and photos of people are copyrighted and carry personality rights. Fine for a
  private concept film; for publishing, license (archival libraries) or replace with generated/own shots.
- Mark every clip in the footage manifest with a `rights` note.

## Fonts and logos
- Commit only fonts whose licence allows redistribution (OFL etc.). Commercial brand fonts stay in the
  brand owner's own copy of `brands/<name>/` — add that folder to `.git/info/exclude` if needed.
- Third-party logos (tickers, partners) only with permission; the stat-card wall uses text tiles by default.
